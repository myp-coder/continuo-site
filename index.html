  • More followers won't fix it.

Questions creators ask

Do I pay anything?

No. No setup fee, no retainer, no hidden costs. We earn a share of revenue, and only once sales come in.

What's the split?

It depends on how much we run for you, and it's agreed in writing before we build anything. Your free plan shows the split we'd propose.

Who owns the product?

Your name, your audience and your content stay yours. Product ownership, and what happens if we ever part ways, are written into the contract before we start.

Do you post on my accounts?

Never. You keep creating the way you do now. We run the backend: the product, checkout, emails, sales calls and support.

How much will I make?

We don't promise numbers, and you should be careful with anyone who does. Your plan shows the assumptions behind every figure, so you can judge them yourself.

How do I get paid?

Sales run through Whop with the revenue split set up from day one, so your share is paid out automatically on every sale.
