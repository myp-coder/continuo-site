Who we are

Continuo is run by Mustafa Yiğit Pektaş in Dubai, United Arab Emirates. He decides how your personal data is used. For anything about your data, email mypektas@continuopartners.com.

What we collect

  • Your answers to the questions on this site: platform, niche, follower range, how often people ask you for advice, where your income comes from and whether you'll go live during launches.
  • Your name, email address and account handle.
  • If you book a call: the details you enter on the booking page, such as your name, email and handle.
  • Messages you send us by email or Instagram.

Why we use it

  • To see whether we're a fit and prepare the plan we bring to your call.
  • To contact you about that call and follow up afterwards.
  • If we work together, to set up and run our agreement.

Under the EU and UK GDPR, we rely on taking steps you've asked for before entering into a contract with you, and on our legitimate interest in answering creators who contact us. We don't sell your data, use it for advertising or make automated decisions about you.

Who handles it for us

  • Google stores your answers, emails and bookings in Google Workspace (Sheets, Gmail and Calendar) under its data processing terms. Google may process data outside your country.
  • GitHub Pages hosts this website. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security.
  • Whop runs payments and payouts if we launch a product together.

Cookies

This site uses no cookies, tracking or analytics. The fonts are served from this site, not from a font service.

How long we keep it

We keep your details for up to 24 months after we were last in touch. If we work together, we keep what the agreement and the law require for as long as they require it.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your data, to restrict or object to how we use it, or to receive a copy of it. Email us and we'll reply within 30 days. You can also complain to the data protection authority where you live. In the UAE, that's the UAE Data Office.
